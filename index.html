Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AI and Human-made services?

Our AI service delivers transcripts and subtitles in under 5 minutes with up to 95% accuracy—ideal for fast drafts, internal notes, and clear recordings. Our Human-made service pairs our AI engine with native language professionals who manually verify every word and timestamp to guarantee 99% broadcast accuracy within 24 hours.

Which export formats does Happy Scribe support?

You can export transcripts and subtitles in SRT, VTT, DOCX, PDF, TXT, STL, Premiere Pro Markers, Final Cut Pro XML, Avid DS, or burn subtitles directly into your video file.

How secure are my uploaded media files?

All files are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Happy Scribe is fully GDPR compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified, and human transcriptionists sign strict NDAs before accessing confidential media.
